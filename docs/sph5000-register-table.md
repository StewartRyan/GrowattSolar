## Settings

These holding registers are configuration entities. A time uses Home Assistant's clock control and is stored as the hour in the high byte and the minute in the low byte. Enables are 0 or 1.

| Address | Entity | What it sets |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1044 | Energy priority | Load first, Battery first, or Grid first |
| 1070 | Discharge power limit | Discharge power during a grid-first window, 0–100% |
| 1071 | Stop discharging at | State of charge where that discharge stops, 0–100% |
| 1080–1088 | Grid-first periods 1–3 | Start, end, and enable for each of the three grid-first windows |
| 1090 | Charge power limit | Charge power during a battery-first window, 0–100% |
| 1091 | Stop charging at | State of charge where that charge stops, 0–100% |
| 1092 | Charge from the grid | Allow grid charging during a battery-first window |
| 1100–1108 | Charge periods 1–3 | Start, end, and enable for each of the three battery-first charge windows |
